A stay + an art gallery

Meet Shivam, the artist host.

Shivam is a photographer and a coffee obsessive, which is exactly what his handle says: clicks and brews. He shoots on a Fujifilm X100VI with a warm, film-like recipe, chasing monsoon streets in Mumbai, golden hour on Marine Drive and, more recently, couples. His first pre-wedding shoot drew over 51,000 likes.

Espresso House is that work, made into a home. His prints hang in the living room and both bedrooms, the coffee corner is his own ritual, and the cafes on the map are the ones he actually goes to. The living room also works as a set for shoots, podcasts and small workshops, with his approval.

  2. 02

    Swirl a little hot water inside, then pour it away.

  3. 03

    Add the coffee

    2 heaped tablespoons for every mug.

  4. 04

    Wet & wait

    Pour a little water to just wet the coffee. Wait 30 seconds.

  5. 05

    Fill, lid on, wait

    Fill up, stir once. Lid on, plunger up. Set a 4 minute timer.

  6. 06

    Press & pour

    Push the plunger down slowly. Pour it all out right away.
